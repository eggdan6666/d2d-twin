 9.2.2.2 Detailed Design Procedure

The circuit in Figure 9-6 employs a ratiometric measurement approach. In other words, the sensor signal (that
 is, the voltage across the RTD in this case) and the reference voltage for the ADC are derived from the same
 excitation source. Therefore, errors resulting from temperature drift or noise of the excitation source cancel
because these errors are common to both the sensor signal and the reference.
