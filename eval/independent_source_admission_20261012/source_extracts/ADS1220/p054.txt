 In some applications, the integrated temperature sensor of the ADS1220 cannot be used (for example,  if the
 accuracy is not high enough or if the device cannot be placed close enough to the cold junction). The additional
 analog input channels of the device can be used in this case to measure the cold-junction temperature with a
 thermistor, RTD, or an analog temperature sensor.

 To get an approximation of the achievable temperature resolution, the rms-Noise of the ADS1220 at Gain =
32 and DR = 20SPS (0.23µVrms) is divided by the average sensitivity of a K-type thermocouple (41µV/°C), as
shown in Equation 17.

     Temperature Resolution = 0.23µV / 41µV/°C = 0.006°C                                                   (17)
